How these estimates work

Let x be your midterm score, y your incomplete online classes, and z your missed in-person classes.

  • Base estimate: 0.60 × x + (11 − y) × (30 / 11) + (13 − z).
  • Likely range: base estimate minus 9 through base estimate plus 2, limited to 0–100.
  • Catch-up time: 1.5 × y hours. Potential additional points: y × (30 / 11), subject to the overall score cap.
  • Maximum scenario: 0.35 × x + 30 + (13 − z) + 25, capped at 100.

The 11 online classes together are worth 30% of your course grade. Each class is worth 30/11 (about 2.73) course points. Calculations use the exact fraction; displayed results are rounded to one decimal place. The supplied weights can total 103 before the score is capped at 100.
